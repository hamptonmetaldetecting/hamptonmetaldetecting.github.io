Landowners

Help us uncover and share the history of our village.

Hampton In Arden Metal Detecting Club is a small local club with a shared interest in discovering, recording and sharing the history beneath our feet.

Much of what we can learn about our local past depends on access to land. By allowing responsible detecting, landowners can help uncover objects that may add another piece to the story of Hampton In Arden and the surrounding area.

Community history

Bringing local history to life.

One of the club's aims is to help people in the village understand more about the history around them — especially younger generations.

Finds can turn history from something read in a book into something tangible: an object lost, carried or used by someone who lived here hundreds, or even thousands, of years ago.

Where appropriate, we hope to record, document and share interesting discoveries so that the wider community can learn from them too.

Our approach

Responsible detecting comes first.

Permission first

We only detect where permission has been given and respect any areas, timings or conditions agreed with the landowner.

Respect the land

Holes are filled carefully and we take care around crops, livestock, boundaries and the wider countryside.

Record responsibly

Finds of potential archaeological importance are handled and recorded through the appropriate channels where required.

What happens next?

A simple conversation to begin with.

There is no obligation. We can start with an informal conversation about your land, the areas that may be suitable and any preferences or conditions you would like us to follow.

Arrangements around access and finds can be discussed and agreed before any detecting takes place.
